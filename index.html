02AI product case study

Rescale OS

AI Creative & Campaign Operations Platform

Senior AI Product Manager, Platform & Agent Systems · Rescale Media LLC
Jan 2026 - Present

Internal platform · Case study available

View Rescale OS case study →
Problem
As creative output grew, production tracking, naming, hand-offs, and ad launches became inconsistent. The core issue was a lack of shared workflow visibility across creative production, review, and launch.
Decision
Chose where AI adds leverage (German translation, 27-field creative analysis) and where humans stay in control. Specified the auto-promotion rules (children launched → Learning; ads paused → Done).
Shipped
Rescale OS models creative work as a lifecycle, not a list of tasks, from concept and script, through rule-generated hook/image child tasks and editing, to launch readiness and the ad launcher.
  • 22+Lifecycle statuses in the creative state machine
  • 27Structured signals from Gemini creative analysis
  • RLSFail-closed workspace isolation
  • Parent/child creative state machine
  • Auto-promotion rules
  • Ad launcher
  • AI-assisted German translation
  • TypeScript monorepo
